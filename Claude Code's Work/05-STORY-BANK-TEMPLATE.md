# 05 · Story Bank

Six stories cover nearly every interview. Most stories prove several things, so tag each one by pillar and skill and you can pull it up fast for a question you didn't expect. In a multi-round process, keep a log of which story you told to whom. Interviewers compare notes.

## 1. Mine your material (10 minutes, in any order)
1. Which 3 results are you proudest of? Include the numbers.
2. Name a hard problem you solved that others couldn't or wouldn't.
3. Name a mistake or failure, and what you changed afterward.
4. When did you learn something new fast and deliver with it?
5. Describe a conflict or disagreement you resolved, and how.
6. When did you lead or influence without having the authority?
7. What has someone praised you for without being asked? Write down their exact words.
8. What unusual personal fact or background would make someone remember you?
9. What 3 traits would colleagues name in you? Ask 2–3 of them. Others often see strengths you discount.

## 2. Six slots to fill
| ID | Slot | Typical questions it answers |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Biggest win (numbers) | Accomplishment, strengths, why hire you |
| S2 | Hard problem / ambiguity | Problem-solving, initiative, pressure |
| S3 | Failure → fix | Weakness, mistake, what you'd do differently |
| S4 | Fast learner | Any gap or "you lack X" objection |
| S5 | People: conflict / influence | Teamwork, a difficult person, leadership |
| S6 | Signature / human | Your EDGE; what makes you memorable |

## 3. Story card (copy six times)
```
ID / TITLE:
PILLAR(S): PROVE / FIT / EDGE        SKILLS (verb+object+manner):
SITUATION (1 line, the pain, no blame):
OBSTACLE (optional, only if you overcame it):
ACTION (I, not we; 2–3 key moves):
RESULT (number + lasting evidence + quote if you have one):
15-SEC VERSION (one sentence):
FEAR IT DISARMS (from 02):
USED WITH (interviewer / round):
```

## 4. Quality check before a story goes in the bank
- [ ] I'm the subject of the action verbs ("I built," not "it happened" or "we did").
- [ ] The result has a number, or a concrete before-and-after.
- [ ] It works in 15 seconds and in 90 seconds.
- [ ] It answers *why this listener should care* (tie it to the JD).
- [ ] It's true and checkable. No inflation, and teammates get credit for their part while you own yours.

## 5. Underqualified? Extend the timeline backward
If you look short on paper, don't inflate. Find the early, concrete origin of the skill (a side project, a family business, a volunteer role, a course you applied right away). That shows years of real interest and competence the title doesn't.
