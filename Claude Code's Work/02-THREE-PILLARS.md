# 02 · The Three Pillars

Every interviewer is quietly scoring three things. Bolles's "5 real questions," Kelley's Competence/Compatibility/Chemistry and the REV filter all come down to these:

| Pillar | Their unspoken question | Your message |
|---|---|---|
| **PROVE** | Can they do the job? | Evidence of results in the core function |
| **FIT** | Will they mesh with us, and do they want *this* job? | Values, work style, real reasons for choosing this company |
| **EDGE** | Why them over 19 (or 900) others? | A trait or story others can't claim |

Repeat these three messages throughout the interview. Interviewers remember a few short phrases, not your whole history, so plant the same three consistently.

## Choose them with the REV filter
List about 10 possible messages from your resume, performance reviews and the questions in 05. Keep only the ones that pass all three tests:
- **Relevant:** matches what *this* job description and employer need. Use their wording.
- **Exceptional:** not a generic claim. "Hard worker," "team player" and "integrity" fail unless you can show they're unusual.
- **Verifiable:** backed by a number, a story or a quote from someone else.

## How to phrase skills
Never state a bare skill. Use verb + object + manner: "negotiating vendor contracts under tight deadlines." Skills support PROVE. Traits (*how* you work) support EDGE.

## Get ahead of their fears
Interviewers watch for signs that you:
- can't do the job
- are unreliable
- will leave soon
- will be slow to ramp up
- won't get along with people
- will do the minimum
- will need constant direction
- have a character flaw
- will embarrass whoever hired you
- will be an expensive mistake

Each pillar story should quietly answer at least one of these fears. Map yours:

## For each job
Re-tune your pillars to each job description. Your stories stay the same; you change which pillar leads. If one pillar is thin, tell your LLM or coach before the interview and prepare the gap script (PIVOT, in 03) for it.
