# Three Pillars: The Messages to Land

## The superstructure
Interviewers should leave able to repeat three simple claims about you. Each pillar is **Need → Proof → Use here**. Repeat them naturally in the opening, examples, close, and follow-up.

| Pillar | What it proves | Build it |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Outcome fit** | You understand the problem and can improve it. | “I improve **[outcome]** by **[method]**; at **[context]** that produced **[result]**. Here I would apply it to **[JD need]**.” |
| **2. Execution fit** | You can do the work in this environment. | “When **[constraint]**, I **[actions]** and **[result]**. That shows I can **[job-critical capability]**.” |
| **3. Growth fit** | You are a credible bet despite the gap. | “I am newer to **[gap]**, but I have learned **[adjacent thing]** by **[evidence]**. My ramp is **[specific plan]**, so I can contribute **[early deliverable]**.” |

## Choose the three
1. Pull the JD apart: required outcomes, hard skills/domain, working style/stakeholders.
2. Assign the strongest available story to each; one story may support two pillars, but do not reuse it word-for-word.
3. Make each claim falsifiable: include a scope, action, result, or concrete learning proof.
4. Write each in **one sentence of 20 words or fewer**.

## Underqualified prioritization
When you cannot win on exact tenure, make the comparison on:
1. **Problem similarity** — same customer, pressure, workflow, or decision even if the industry differs.
2. **Transferable execution** — research, prioritization, stakeholder management, analysis, selling, operations, or delivery.
3. **Learning velocity** — a past instance of gaining an unfamiliar tool/domain and producing useful work.
4. **Risk reduction** — a realistic first-30-days plan, not a promise to be expert on day one.

## Repetition map
- **Opening:** state all three in compressed form.
- **Experience question:** make the relevant pillar the final line.
- **Weakness/gap question:** reinforce Pillar 3.
- **“Why us?”:** reinforce Pillar 1.
- **Closing:** “The three things I hope you take away are…” then state them.

## Avoid
- Three personality adjectives without evidence.
- Three unrelated achievements.
- A defensive “I know I lack…” narrative.
- Claiming the company’s problem is solved before asking questions.
