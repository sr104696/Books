# Reusable Story Bank

Build six stories before interviews. They may come from paid work, school, community work, projects, caregiving, sports, or self-directed learning—provided the account is accurate and your role is clear.

## Story coverage map
| # | Story type | Best questions it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Biggest relevant result | achievement, strength, tell me about yourself |
| 2 | Hard problem / ambiguity | problem-solving, prioritization, judgment |
| 3 | Collaboration / conflict | teamwork, communication, influence |
| 4 | Leadership / initiative | leadership, ownership, working without authority |
| 5 | Failure / feedback / recovery | weakness, resilience, self-awareness |
| 6 | Learned something fast / transition | gap, adaptability, technical learning |

## STAR+R card (copy six times)
### [Title: action + outcome]
- **Pillar supported:** [1 / 2 / 3]
- **Question triggers:** [e.g., conflict, deadline, leadership]
- **S — Situation:** What was happening? Include only context needed to understand the stakes.
- **T — Task:** What specifically were *you* accountable for? What did success require?
- **A — Actions:** List 2–3 decisions/actions in order. Include how you worked with others.
  1. …
  2. …
  3. …
- **R — Result:** What changed? Use a number, deadline, quality indicator, stakeholder reaction, or concrete output. Distinguish team result from your contribution.
- **R — Relevance:** “This demonstrates **[capability]**, which I would use here to **[JD outcome]**.”
- **Gap bridge (if needed):** “While the context differs in **[domain/tool]**, the same underlying work was **[similarity]**.”
- **Evidence check:** What can I substantiate? What must I qualify?
- **20-second version:** [headline + result]
- **90-second version:** [H-E-A-R response]

## Story quality test
Keep a story if it contains: a real obstacle, your distinct choices, an outcome, and a capability relevant to the target role. Replace stories that are only duties, rescue fantasies, blame narratives, or team wins with no stated contribution.
