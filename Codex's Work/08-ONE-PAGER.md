# Interview Control One-Pager

## Before the interview
- **Role outcome:** ________________________________
- **Problem / constraint:** _________________________
- **Stakeholders:** _________________________________
- **My honest gap:** ________________________________
- **My ramp proof + first step:** ____________________

## My 60-second opening
> I help **[who]** achieve **[outcome]**. In **[context]**, I **[action]** and **[result]**. I am strong at **[capability]** and **[capability]**. This role matters because **[need]**; I would apply that to **[contribution]**.

## Three pillars
1. **Outcome:** I improve __________________ by __________________; proof: __________________.
2. **Execution:** I can __________________ under __________________; proof: __________________.
3. **Growth:** I have not yet __________________, but I learned/delivered __________________; ramp: __________________.

## Answer engine
- **Behavioral:** **H-E-A-R** = Headline → Environment/stakes → Actions → Result + relevance.
- **Unknown scenario:** **C-A-R-R** = Clarify → Approach → Risk-check → Result/measure.
- **Gap:** **G-A-P** = Gap → Adjacent proof → Plan.
- **Motivation/opinion:** **P-O-V** = Position → One example → Value here.

## Control lines
- “Are you asking about **A** or **B**?”
- “Let me choose the most relevant example.”
- “I have not done that exact thing; the closest comparable work was…”
- “The key point is…”
- “The part most relevant here is…”

> “The three things I hope you take away are **[pillar 1]**, **[pillar 2]**, and **[pillar 3]**. What would make the first six months a clear success?”
