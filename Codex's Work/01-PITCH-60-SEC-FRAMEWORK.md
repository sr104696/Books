# 60-Second Pitch Framework

## Target: 45–75 seconds
**Rule:** lead with the employer’s need, prove only 1–2 claims, and finish with a forward-looking fit. Do not recite chronology or every resume bullet.

## Formula A — Direct match
> “I’m a **[role/functional identity]** who helps **[customer/team]** achieve **[outcome]**. In **[recent context]**, I **[action]**, which led to **[specific result]**. I’m especially strong at **[capability 1]** and **[capability 2]**. This role stood out because **[company problem/opportunity]**; I’d bring that same approach to **[first relevant contribution]**.”

## Formula B — Underqualified / adjacent move
> “My background is primarily in **[adjacent field/context]**, where the recurring problem was **[same underlying problem]**. For example, I **[action]** and **[result]** by using **[transferable capability]**. I have not yet **[honest gap]**, so I would not overstate that. What I can show is **[proof of learning/adjacent delivery]**, and I have a focused ramp plan: **[domain learning + early deliverable]**. That is why I believe I can help with **[role outcome]**.”

## Formula C — Early career / sparse experience
> “I’m building my career in **[target area]**. Across **[coursework / internship / project / service / work]**, I repeatedly took on **[relevant responsibility]**. In one example, I **[action]**, resulting in **[result/evidence]**. The pattern is that I **[strength]** when faced with **[problem]**. I’m looking for a role where I can apply that foundation to **[job outcome]** and learn **[specific domain]** quickly.”

## Formula D — Career change
> “I’m moving from **[past field]** toward **[target field]** because I do my best work when **[relevant motivation/problem]**. My prior work gives me a useful advantage: I have **[capability]** in **[context]**, demonstrated when I **[action/result]**. I’ve already closed part of the transition gap through **[course/project/portfolio/volunteer work]**. I’m targeting this role because **[specific need]**, where I can contribute **[immediate value]** while building **[missing domain skill]**.”

## Build sheet
- **Need:** What must improve if this person is hired?
- **Identity:** What useful problem-solver label fits you?
- **Proof:** One result with scope, number, deadline, quality, or stakeholder evidence.
- **Two capabilities:** Name verbs, not traits: e.g., diagnose, organize, influence, analyze, build, improve.
- **Bridge:** What is adjacent—not identical—about your evidence?
- **Forward use:** What would you investigate or deliver first?

## Delivery checks
- Say “I” for your contribution and “we” for team context.
- Substitute evidence for labels: not “results-oriented”; say what changed.
- Mention the gap once, plainly; spend more time on proof and ramp.
- End cleanly. Then ask: “Would it be useful to go deeper on that example or on how I would approach the first 90 days?”
