# Distilled Interview & Career Pitch Playbook

## Overview & Philosophy
This repository contains a distilled, high-impact interview system synthesized from top career, interview, and self-advocacy source materials (including Andrew LaCivita's *Interview Intervention*, Peggy Klaus's *Brag!*, Ron Fry's *101 Great Answers*, John Kador's *301 Best Questions to Ask*, Richard Nelson Bolles's *What Color Is Your Parachute?*, and *Get That Job!*).

### What Was Kept vs. Culled
- **Culled (Discarded):** Motivation fluff, generic job-search encouragement, repetitive low-value Q&A lists (e.g., 50 variations of "where do you see yourself in 5 years?"), outdated resume formatting rules, and passive reactive advice.
- **Kept & Grafted:** Strategic narrative frameworks, positioning mechanics, brag-bites / value-pillar formulas, universal answer superstructures, bridging tactics for taking control (especially when underqualified), and reusable story architectures.

### How to Use This Toolkit
1. **Immediate Prep (15-Minute Sprint):** Review `08-ONE-PAGER.md`, draft your 3 Pillars using `02-THREE-PILLARS.md`, and pick 1 pitch formula from `01-PITCH-60-SEC-FRAMEWORK.md`.
2. **Comprehensive Prep (1-2 Hours):** Fill out `05-STORY-BANK-TEMPLATE.md` with 5-6 modular STAR stories, review `03-UNIVERSAL-ANSWER-SUPERSTRUCTURES.md` and `06-CONTROL-BRIDGING-TACTICS.md` to handle curveballs and objections.
3. **Automated Personalization via LLM:** Open `07-LLM-MASTER-PROMPT.md`, paste the prompt along with these markdown files, your Resume, and the Target Job Description into ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini to generate custom, targeted preparation materials instantly.

## Core Rule of Engagement
**Never be passive.** An interview is not an interrogation; it is a collaborative business conversation. Your goal is to pivot every question back to your **3 Value Pillars** using **Universal Superstructures** and **Control Bridging Tactics**.
