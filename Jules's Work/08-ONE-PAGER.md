# Emergency 1-Pager: Interview Cheat Sheet

*Keep this document open for quick reference 15 minutes before and during your interview.*

---

## 1. Golden Rules of Engagement
- **Control the Narrative:** An interview is a business conversation, not an interrogation.
- **Brag with Facts:** Use metrics, percentages, dollar amounts, and time saved. Never talk in vague generalities.
- **Rule of Ownership:** Always describe your specific contribution using **"I"**, not "We".
- **The 60-Second Stop Rule:** Keep responses under 60 seconds. End with a check-in question to maintain control.

---

## 2. Your 60-Second Elevating Pitch Structure
```text
[PRESENT] "I am a [Current Role] specializing in [Core Superpower/Domain]..."
[PAST]    "Over the past [X] years, I've [Major Achievement 1] and [Major Achievement 2 with Metric]..."
[FUTURE]  "What excites me about [Company] is [Specific Need/Goal], and I'm here to bring my [Pillar 1 Skill] to drive [Target Result]."
```

---

## 3. The 3 Value Pillars (Your Baseline Takeaways)
Before answering, ask yourself: *"Which pillar am I reinforcing?"*
- **Pillar 1 (Hard Skills):** Technical / Domain Mastery + Metric Win.
- **Pillar 2 (Execution):** Process, System-Thinking & Crisis Resolution.
- **Pillar 3 (Leadership/Adaptability):** Stakeholder Alignment, Rapid Learning & EQ.

---

## 4. Emergency Answer Superstructures

### Option A: Behavioral / Past Story (PARL)
- **P - Problem (20%):** High-stakes business context.
- **A - Action (40%):** Step 1, Step 2, Step 3 that **YOU** took.
- **R - Result (20%):** Quantified outcome (% growth, time saved, $ created).
- **L - Learning (20%):** Strategic insight you apply today.

### Option B: Handling Missing Skill / Weakness / Objection (PPPV)
- **P - Premise:** Acknowledge directly (*"I don't have 5 years in X..."*).
- **P - Pivot:** Shift to core capability (*"...however, my mastery of Y means..."*).
- **P - Proof:** Give a 30-sec example of rapid mastery (*"For example, when..."*).
- **V - Value:** Reframe edge for employer (*"Because of this, I bring fresh perspective..."*).

### Option C: Off-Guard / Unknown Question (CBD)
- **C - Clarify:** Restate/boundary check (*"To clarify, are you asking about X or Y?"*).
- **B - Bridge:** Connect to Pillar (*"That touches on how I manage [Pillar 2]..."*).
- **D - Deliver:** Give 3 quick steps + Check-in.

---

## 5. Quick Bridging Phrases (When Stuck or Underqualified)
- *"While I haven't used [Tool A] directly, my mastery of [Tool B] means..."*
- *"Coming from outside [Industry X], my edge is bringing battle-tested [Skill Y]..."*
- *"I measure experience by impact rather than years spent. For example..."*
- *"I don't have that exact syntax memorized, but here is my 3-step logic to solve it..."*

---

## 6. Closing Check-Ins (Never End with Awkward Silence)
- *"Does that align with how your team approaches [Topic]?"*
- *"I can pause there or dive deeper into the technical architecture—where would you like to go next?"*

---

## 7. High-Impact Questions to Ask Them
1. *"What does winning in this role look like in the first 90 days, and what is the #1 bottleneck holding the team back right now?"*
2. *"If we look back 1 year from today and agree this was a stellar hire, what specific metric did we move?"*
