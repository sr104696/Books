# 60-Second Elevating Pitch Formulas

When asked *"Tell me about yourself"* or *"Walk me through your resume"*, your goal is not to recite a chronological resume, but to deliver a focused 60-second narrative that establishes your value proposition and anchors your 3 Pillars.

---

## Formula 1: The Standard "Present-Past-Future" Model

**Best For:** Standard interview loops where you meet core qualifications.
**Time Target:** 45-60 seconds (approx. 130-150 words).

### Structure
1. **Present (The Hook):** Current title, core role, and overarching superpower or mandate.
2. **Past (The Proof):** 2 quick achievements or experiences that prove your track record.
3. **Future (The Fit):** Why this specific role/company is the exact next step where you add immediate value.

### Fill-in-the-Blank Template
> "I am currently a **[Current Title/Role]** specializing in **[Core Area of Expertise]**, where my main focus is **[Primary High-Value Function]**.
> Over the past **[Number]** years, I've built a track record of **[Major Competency #1]** and **[Major Competency #2]**—most recently **[Brief Specific Metric/Impact Achieved]**.
> What drew me to **[Target Company]** is **[Specific Strategic Goal or Need of Target Company]**. I'm excited about this role because it sits right at the intersection of my background in **[Key Skill]** and my ability to **[Value Proposition for Target Role]**."

---

## Formula 2: The "Problem-Solution-Impact" Value Pitch

**Best For:** Senior roles, turnarounds, or positions created to solve an explicit business pain point.
**Time Target:** 60 seconds.

### Structure
1. **The Core Problem:** Identify the exact type of business problem you exist to solve.
2. **Your Unique Solution:** How you approach and resolve this problem systematically.
3. **The Track Record / Impact:** Proven metrics demonstrating success.
4. **The Target Application:** How you will apply this framework to the employer's current needs.

### Fill-in-the-Blank Template
> "At my core, I am a business problem solver who helps organizations overcome **[Target Company's Key Pain Point, e.g., scaling operational bottlenecks / low user retention]**.
> My approach combines **[Methodology/Pillar 1]** with **[Methodology/Pillar 2]**.
> For example, at **[Previous Company/Project]**, I encountered **[Specific Business Challenge]** and deployed **[Key Action]**, which resulted in **[Quantifiable Impact, e.g., 35% growth / $2M saved]**.
> I see **[Target Company]** undergoing **[Target Company Challenge/Opportunity]**, and I'm here to bring that exact same structured execution to drive **[Desired Business Result]**."

---

## Formula 3: The Underqualified / Career Pivot Pitch

**Best For:** Pivoting industries, changing roles, or when you do not meet all formal requirements.
**Time Target:** 60 seconds.

### Core Strategy: Reframing Underqualification
Do not apologize for missing checkboxes. Frame your unconventional background as your **primary competitive advantage** (bringing fresh perspectives, cross-domain agility, and accelerated adaptability).

### Structure
1. **The Anchor:** Your core identity grounded in transferable high-value skills.
2. **The Common Thread:** The underlying theme connecting your previous career to this new role.
3. **The Catalyst & Transferable Proof:** Why you are making this move now and how your past achievements guarantee success in the new domain.
4. **The Unfair Advantage:** Why an outsider/cross-functional candidate delivers superior value over traditional candidates.

### Fill-in-the-Blank Template
> "Throughout my career, the common thread driving my success has been **[Core Transferable Skill, e.g., data-driven decision making / leading crisis turnarounds]**.
> While my background spans **[Previous Industry/Domain]**, my primary value has always been **[Core Business Result, e.g., transforming complex technical problems into executable strategy]**.
> For instance, at **[Previous Role]**, when faced with **[Unfamiliar Scenario]**, I rapidly **[Action Taken]**, achieving **[Quantifiable Result]**.
> While I come from a slightly different background than a traditional **[Target Role Title]**, that is precisely my edge: I bring **[Unique Perspective/Skill from Previous Domain]** paired with an outsider's agility. I can hit the ground running to tackle **[Key Target Challenge]** without legacy biases."

---

## Delivery Execution Rules
- **The 60-Second Stop Rule:** Pause at the 60-second mark and check in: *"I can pause there, or dive deeper into any of those areas—where would you like to explore first?"* (This asserts conversational control and prevents rambling).
- **Tone:** Energetic, concise, confident ("Bragging with facts, not fluff").
