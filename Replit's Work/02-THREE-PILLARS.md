# 02 — Three Pillars

LaCivita’s direct model says hiring depends on three factors. The candidate
controls the first most directly and can improve the other two.

## 1. Encoding → articulate value

State qualifications and potential contributions in plain, role-specific
language. Use three to five REV points:

- **Relevant** to the job’s requirements or needs.
- **Exceptional** versus other qualified candidates.
- **Verifiable** through evidence.

**Move:** lead, answer, or close with a REV point rather than hoping the
interviewer discovers it in the résumé. (LaCivita; Kelley)

## 2. Decoding → make value understood correctly

Interviewers bring assumptions, incomplete information, and different
perspectives. Reduce the gap:

1. Identify what the question is trying to assess.
2. Clarify one ambiguous term or assumption.
3. Answer in the requested form (past story or future approach).
4. Check the connection: “Is that the kind of example you had in mind?”
5. Link the evidence to the stated requirement.

**Move:** ask about priorities and expectations, then use the answer to
select your most relevant proof. (LaCivita; Bolles; Kador)

## 3. Memory → remain recallable

Make the interviewer’s later summary easy:

- Repeat the same few REV themes across answers.
- Use specific, vivid SOAR stories and results.
- Quantify impact or give concrete evidence.
- Use short, memorable descriptions of your working style.
- Start and finish with the strongest points.
- Close with a concise recap and interest statement.

LaCivita explains that later recall compresses the interview into impressions;
Kelley and Klaus emphasize memorable stories and coherent self-presentation.

## The operational loop (synthesis)

**Value → Evidence → Direction**

1. **Value:** what relevant difference can you make?
2. **Evidence:** when have you made that difference?
3. **Direction:** what does the employer need next, and what question or
   bridge moves the conversation there?

When underqualified, do not hide the gap. Make the loop: “I have not yet
done [exact requirement]. I have done [adjacent capability] in [evidence],
and that is how I would approach learning/contributing here.” This is a
truthful synthesis of REV, SOAR, and the sources’ emphasis on future
contribution—not a direct quotation from any one book.
