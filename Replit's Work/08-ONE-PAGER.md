# Interview Control — One-Page Cheat Sheet

## 1. Set the message

Pick **3 REV points**:

**Relevant** to the role · **Exceptional** versus peers · **Verifiable** by
results, facts, recognition, or stories. (Kelley)

## 2. Open in 60 seconds

**Who I am → Point + proof → Point + proof → transfer to need → question**

“I’m a [role/domain] who has [summary]. I bring [point], shown by [result].
Also, [point], shown by [result]. That maps to your need for [requirement]
because [connection]. Which area would be most useful to explore?”

Use talking points, not a script. (Kelley; Fry; Klaus)

## 3. Decode every question

**DID?** Past behavior → SOAR.  
**WOULD?** Hypothetical → assumptions → approach → success measure.
(LaCivita; Fry)

Underlying concern (Bolles): **Why here? What can you do? What kind of
person? What distinguishes you? Can we afford you?**

## 4. Answer with proof

**Headline → Situation/pain → Action (I) → Result → relevance → hand-off**

Keep context short. Quantify. Obstacles are optional. Most answers under one
minute; stories can expand to two. (Kelley)

## 5. If underqualified

“I have not done [exact requirement]. The closest evidence is [situation],
where I [transferable action] and achieved [result]. I would apply that
approach to [this need], while learning [specific missing area].”

Use only truthful evidence; never substitute confidence for proof.

## 6. Bridge and steer

- “The most relevant example is…”
- “That result demonstrates [capability], which matters here because…”
- “Would you like the short version or more detail?”
- Ask short, open questions. Questions can steer the conversation. (Kador)

## 7. Ask in order

**Company:** priorities, challenges, growth, contribution.  
**Role:** top three priorities, success measures, top-performer skills.  
**Boss:** expectations, management style, working relationship.
(LaCivita; Kador)

## 8. Close

**Confirm:** “You need [skills] for [responsibilities], correct?”  
**Assure:** “My evidence in [stories/results] matches those needs.”  
**Close:** “I’m very interested. Any reservations? What is the next step?”

Clarify misunderstandings, fill blind spots, address valid gaps honestly.
(LaCivita; Kador)

## Three pillars to remember

**Encoding:** say your value.  
**Decoding:** make it understood.  
**Memory:** make it recallable.  
(LaCivita; operationalized with Kelley’s REV + SOAR and source question
mechanics.)
