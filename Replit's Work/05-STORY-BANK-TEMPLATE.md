# 05 — Story Bank Template

Build a bank, not a script. Kelley calls the core format **SOAR**; Fry’s
PAR/CAR variants are equivalent. Use STAR if requested, with Obstacles as an
optional addition.

## Story card (duplicate for each story)

**Title:**  
Specific and searchable: `[outcome] — [context/date]`

**Maps to:**  
- REV point(s):  
- Job requirement(s):  
- Question(s):  
- Alternate story options:

**S — Situation (brief context/pain):**  
Role, time, setting, and what was at risk or missing.

**O — Obstacles (optional):**  
Constraint, resistance, deadline, scarce resource, or risk. Include only if
it makes your action more impressive.

**A — Actions (my contribution):**  
Three to five decision/action prompts. Make “I” clear inside any team story.

**R — Results (specific evidence):**  
Metric (time, money, quality, revenue, risk, customer, team), recognition,
adoption, or lasting process change.

**What this demonstrates:**  
Technical skills; judgment; initiative; collaboration; leadership; learning;
resilience; communication; or other supported strengths.

**Versions:**  
- 15 seconds: situation → action → result  
- 60 seconds: full SOAR with relevance  
- Expanded: details only if asked

## Build the bank

1. Start with every REV point.
2. Mine job postings: for each requirement, ask when you demonstrated it.
3. Mine the résumé, LinkedIn, reviews, kudos, awards, and feedback.
4. Practice behavioral questions; add a card whenever a story appears.
5. Target **20 or more** stories for multiple interviews (Kelley); LaCivita’s
   example uses 20–30.
6. Map two or three possible stories to each important question. Do not repeat
   one story across every interviewer.

## Quality check

- Situation is short; Actions and Results carry the weight.
- Result is quantified where possible; otherwise use concrete evidence.
- No blame, inflated claims, or “we” that hides your role.
- Story genuinely answers the question and supports a REV point.
- The 15-second version is actually timed aloud.
- The interviewer can see the connection to this role.

## Story selection cue

When a question arrives, search by **skill and need**, not by its exact
wording. A story can demonstrate multiple skills, so list every truthful
mapping on the card. (Kelley)

**Source:** direct Kelley SOAR, flashcard, 15-second, and 20-plus-story
mechanics; direct Fry behavioral evidence principle; title/mapping layout is
toolkit synthesis.
