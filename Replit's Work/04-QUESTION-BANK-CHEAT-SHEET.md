# 04 — Question Bank Cheat Sheet

Prepare questions to learn whether the job fits and to make your value
visible. Use short, open-ended, one-point questions. (Kador)

## Interviewer’s likely questions: prepare evidence

| Meta-category | Typical wording | Prepare |
|---|---|---|
| Why here? | Why this job/company? What do you know about us? | Research-based reason + relevant REV point |
| Capability | What can you do for us? Greatest strengths? | REV point + SOAR proof |
| Person/fit | How do you work with others? Management style? | principle + story showing behavior |
| Distinction | Why should we hire you? What makes you different? | differentiated, verifiable point |
| Future | Where do you see yourself developing? | contribution and learning direction |
| Risk | Weakness, failure, gap, job change | brief fact + action + improved result |
| Past behavior | Tell me about a time… | SOAR story, “I” ownership, result |
| Hypothetical | What would you do if…? | assumptions + approach + success measure |

These collapse common lists into Bolles’s five underlying employer questions
and LaCivita’s did/would types.

## Questions for the employer: Company → Role → Boss

### Company

- What are the company’s most important priorities or challenges now?
- What would successful growth or progress look like?
- How does this team contribute to the wider organization?

### Role

- What are the top three priorities for this position initially?
- How will you judge success?
- What should be accomplished a year from now?
- Which skills does a top performer use most in this role?

### Boss / interviewer

- How would you describe your management style and expectations?
- What does a strong working relationship look like to you?
- Where could the team use some improvement or support?

LaCivita recommends the Company → Role → Boss sequence. Adapt questions to
the interviewer and remove anything already answered.

## High-value closing questions

- “Is there anything I can clarify about my experience?”
- “Do you have any concerns about my fulfilling this position?”
- “Based on what we discussed, what would be the next step?”
- “Everything I have heard reinforces my interest. Is there anything else
  you need in deciding whether I am a fit?”

Kador and LaCivita support inviting objections; a stated concern can be
clarified, supplied with missing evidence, or addressed honestly.

## Rules

- Prefer “how,” “what,” and “which” over yes/no questions.
- Keep each question to one point.
- Ask to understand expectations, not to launch into compensation or
  “what about me?” topics prematurely. (Kador)
- Write questions down and prioritize a “killer question” in case time ends.
- The best killer question combines the employer’s challenge, your ability
  to help, and a request for the opportunity. (Kador)

**Source:** direct synthesis of Kador’s question rules, LaCivita’s
Company/Role/Boss buckets, and Bolles’s candidate-side mirror questions.
