# Three Pillars Framework

## Concept

Your **3 Pillars** are the core messages you want every interviewer to remember. Every answer should tie back to at least one pillar. Think of them as your campaign platform.

**Also known as:** REV Points (Relevant, Exceptional, Verifiable), Value Pillars, Core Messages, Positioning Themes

---

## The REV Criteria

Each pillar must be:

### **R**elevant
- Directly addresses job requirements
- Solves employer's pain points  
- Matches language in job description

### **E**xceptional  
- Distinguishes you from other candidates
- Not generic ("hard worker," "team player")
- Quantifiable or rare

### **V**erifiable
- You can prove it with examples
- Backed by metrics or concrete outcomes
- Third-party validation (awards, reviews, promotions)

---

## How to Develop Your 3 Pillars

### Step 1: Brain Dump (10 min)
List 10-15 reasons an employer should hire you:
- Technical skills
- Soft skills
- Accomplishments
- Awards/recognition
- Unique experiences
- Domain knowledge
- Specialized training
- Track record

### Step 2: Match to Job (5 min)
For each item, ask:
- Does job description mention this directly?
- Does this solve a pain point they have?
- Is this top 3 priority or nice-to-have?

Highlight top 5 matches.

### Step 3: Apply REV Filter (5 min)
Score each on 1-3 scale:
- **Relevant** (job requires it)
- **Exceptional** (rare/impressive)  
- **Verifiable** (you have proof)

Pick 3-5 with highest combined scores.

### Step 4: Refine Language (5 min)
Turn each into a memorable phrase:
- Use action words
- Include specificity
- Make it conversational
- Add metrics when possible

## Examples

### Example 1: Software Engineer Role

**Job Requirement:** "Build scalable systems for high-traffic applications"

**Your Pillars:**

1. **"I've scaled systems from 10K to 5M daily users"**
   - Relevant: Exactly what job needs
   - Exceptional: 500x scale is rare
   - Verifiable: "At Company X, I redesigned architecture that..."

2. **"I specialize in performance optimization under constraints"**
   - Relevant: High-traffic = performance critical
   - Exceptional: Most devs don't focus on this
   - Verifiable: "Reduced API latency by 73% while cutting costs 40%"

3. **"I lead cross-functional teams through technical complexity"**
   - Relevant: Senior role requires leadership
   - Exceptional: Junior devs can't claim this
   - Verifiable: "Led squad of 5 through migration, zero downtime"

### Example 2: Marketing Manager (Underqualified)

**Job Requirement:** "5 years experience, B2B SaaS, demand gen"

**Your Pillars (you have 2 years B2C):**

1. **"I've run full-funnel campaigns that converted at 2x industry average"**
   - Relevant: They need conversion results
   - Exceptional: 2x is impressive regardless of domain
   - Verifiable: "Campaign drove 850 MQLs, 18% became customers"

2. **"I bring fresh B2C tactics that B2B companies are just discovering"**
   - Relevant: Solves "stuck in old playbook" problem
   - Exceptional: Most B2B candidates don't have this
   - Verifiable: "Implemented influencer strategy that... [results]"

3. **"I've managed $500K budgets and made every dollar accountable"**
   - Relevant: Manager role needs budget responsibility
   - Exceptional: Shows trust and impact
   - Verifiable: "Achieved $1.8M pipeline on $500K spend = 3.6x ROI"

### Example 3: Recent Grad

**Job Requirement:** "Entry-level analyst, data analysis, Excel/SQL"

**Your Pillars:**

1. **"I've already done professional-level analytics work"**
   - Relevant: They want Day 1 productivity
   - Exceptional: Most grads only have coursework
   - Verifiable: "Internship project saved company $40K annually"

2. **"I'm certified in tools most entry-level candidates don't have"**
   - Relevant: Shows initiative and capability
   - Exceptional: Certification > just coursework
   - Verifiable: "Tableau Desktop Specialist, AWS Cloud Practitioner"

3. **"I thrive on turning ambiguous problems into clear insights"**
   - Relevant: Analysts face unclear requirements
   - Exceptional: Most grads struggle with ambiguity
   - Verifiable: "Capstone: analyzed messy dataset, found X, recommended Y"

## Pillar Templates by Situation

### For Technical Roles:
- "I've built/shipped [X] that handled [scale/complexity]"
- "I specialize in [specific technology/domain] under [constraint]"
- "I've reduced [problem] by [metric] through [approach]"

### For Leadership Roles:
- "I've led [team size] through [difficult transition/project]"
- "I grew [metric] by [X%] in [timeframe]"
- "I've built [process/team/function] from zero to [result]"

### For Pivots/Career Changes:
- "I bring [transferable skill] from [previous domain] that [advantage]"
- "I've already proven I can [relevant task] through [recent evidence]"
- "My [unique background] gives me [perspective] that [benefit to company]"

### For Underqualified:
- "I've achieved [exceptional result] despite [having less experience/resources]"
- "I learn [new skill type] exceptionally fast—for example, [proof]"
- "I've done [advanced thing] that candidates with more years often haven't"

## Usage Strategy

### In Opening (First 5 Minutes):
Lead with all 3 pillars in your 60-second pitch.

**Example:**
"For the past 3 years I've been [role], and what distinguishes me is:
- [Pillar 1]
- [Pillar 2]  
- [Pillar 3]

For example, [15-second story proving one pillar]."

### In Answers (Throughout Interview):
Every answer should reinforce at least ONE pillar.

**Weak Answer:**
"Yes, I'm familiar with agile methodology. We used it at my last job."

**Strong Answer (Pillar-Reinforcing):**
"Absolutely. At Company X, I **led cross-functional teams through technical complexity** [Pillar 3] using agile. Specifically, we ran 2-week sprints that **scaled the system from 10K to 5M users** [Pillar 1] with zero regressions."

### In Closing:
Restate 2-3 pillars before asking for next steps.

**Example:**
"Based on our conversation, I'm confident my experience **scaling high-traffic systems** [Pillar 1] and **optimizing performance** [Pillar 2] would make an immediate impact. What are the next steps?"

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Too Generic
**Bad:** "I'm a hard worker"  
**Good:** "I shipped 3 features ahead of schedule last quarter"

### ❌ Not Verifiable
**Bad:** "I'm great with people"  
**Good:** "I raised our NPS from 42 to 78 in 6 months"

### ❌ Irrelevant to Job
**Bad:** "I'm fluent in Spanish" (for role that doesn't need it)  
**Good:** "I've managed remote teams across time zones"

### ❌ Too Many Pillars
**Bad:** Listing 7-8 things (dilutes message)  
**Good:** 3-5 maximum (memorable and repeatable)

## Testing Your Pillars

**The Handshake Test:**
Could you confidently say all 3 pillars in 30 seconds when shaking hands at a networking event?

**The Recall Test:**
Would interviewer remember your 3 pillars 24 hours later?

**The Differentiation Test:**
Could these pillars apply to 10 other candidates? If yes, they're too generic.

**The Proof Test:**
Can you tell a 30-second story proving each pillar?

---

## Summary Formula

```
3-5 PILLARS × REV CRITERIA × PROOF STORIES = MEMORABLE POSITIONING

Each pillar should be:
- 1 sentence or short phrase
- Quantified when possible
- Backed by 1-2 SOAR stories
- Repeated 3+ times during interview
```

Your pillars are your campaign. Run on them. Win on them.
