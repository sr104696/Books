# ONE-PAGER: Emergency Interview Cheat Sheet

*Print this. Keep it visible 15 minutes before interview. Scan it during bathroom breaks.*

---

## THE 4 GOLDEN RULES

1. **Control the narrative** → Interviews are conversations, not interrogations
2. **Brag with facts** → Use metrics, percentages, dollar amounts (never vague)
3. **Say "I" not "we"** → Your specific contribution and decisions
4. **60-second rule** → Most answers under 60 sec; complex stories max 2 min

---

## YOUR 60-SECOND PITCH STRUCTURE

```
[10s] HOOK: "For past [X yrs] I've been [role], specializing in [top strength]"
[30s] PILLARS: List 3 selling points with brief proof
[15s] STORY: One 15-sec example with quantified result
[10s] HUMANITY: What drives you / why this role
[5s] HANDOFF: "How does that align with what you're looking for?"
```

**Practice until you can say it naturally in 70 seconds.**

## 4 UNIVERSAL ANSWER STRUCTURES

### Structure 1: SOAR (For "Tell me about a time...")
```
S - Situation: Company, role, pain point (10s)
O - Obstacles: What made it hard (5s)
A - Actions: What YOU did, step by step (30s)
R - Results: Quantified impact (15s)
```

### Structure 2: SANDWICH (For weaknesses/failures)
```
POSITIVE: Growth mindset frame (15s)
NEGATIVE: Brief honest weakness (20s)
POSITIVE: What you learned + how you've improved (25s)
```

### Structure 3: PILLAR + PROOF (For strengths)
```
State pillar (10s) → Give SOAR story (40s) → Tie to job (10s)
```

### Structure 4: BRIDGE + REFRAME (For objections)
```
Acknowledge gap (5s) → Bridge phrase (5s) → Shift to strength (50s)
```

## BRIDGE PHRASES (When Caught Off Guard)

Copy these to notecard:

- "While I'm developing X, where I excel is Y..."
- "Coming from [other domain], my edge is..."
- "I measure experience by outcomes, not years..."
- "I don't have direct experience, but here's my approach..."
- "What IS relevant is..."
- "Speaking of that, what's more important is..."
- "That reminds me of when I..."

## 8 QUESTION TYPES → 8 STRUCTURES

| Question Type | Use Structure | Key Tactic |
|---------------|---------------|------------|
| "Tell me about a time..." | SOAR | Use "I", add obstacles, quantify results |
| "What are your strengths?" | Pillar + Proof | Match to job requirements |
| "Why do you want this job?" | Values + Example | Show research, genuine interest |
| "What's your weakness?" | Sandwich | Already corrected OR irrelevant to job |
| "You lack X" | Bridge + Reframe | Acknowledge → pivot to related strength |
| "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?" | Past + Future | Ambitious but realistic |
| "How would you handle X?" | Framework + Example | Show thinking process |
| "Do you have questions?" | Strategic Questions | See below |

## 5 KILLER QUESTIONS TO ASK THEM

**Red Flag Question (address objections):**
"Do you have any concerns about my background for this role?"

**Expectations Question:**
"How will you measure my success in the first 90 days?"

**Challenge Question:**
"What's the biggest challenge your team is facing right now?"

**Culture Question:**
"What do people who succeed here have in common?"

**Close Question:**
"Based on our conversation, what are the next steps?"

---

## CONTROL TACTICS

### Tactic 1: End Every Answer with Check-In
"Does that match how your team approaches [topic]?"  
"Would you like me to expand on that, or should we move on?"

### Tactic 2: Preemptive Objection Nuke
If you have obvious gap, address it in opening pitch BEFORE they ask

### Tactic 3: Set Agenda in First 5 Minutes
Use "Tell me about yourself" to establish YOUR 3 pillars

### Tactic 4: Circle Back to Weak Answers
"Earlier you asked about X—let me add something I should've mentioned..."

---

## RED FLAGS TO AVOID

- ❌ Being late (no excuses accepted)
- ❌ Using "we" instead of "I"
- ❌ Rambling past 2 minutes
- ❌ Badmouthing former employers
- ❌ Not asking any questions
- ❌ Phone on / checking time
- ❌ Weak handshake / poor eye contact
- ❌ Answering question they didn't ask
- ❌ Creating negative sound bites ("I've never...")
- ❌ Lying about anything (automatic termination)

## OPENING SEQUENCE (First 5 Min)

1. Warm greeting + firm handshake
2. Wait for "Tell me about yourself"
3. Deliver 60-second pitch with 3 pillars
4. End with: "How does that align with what you're looking for?"
5. **LISTEN** to their priorities—adjust rest of interview to emphasize those

---

## CLOSING SEQUENCE (Last 5 Min)

1. Ask red flag question: "Any concerns about my fit?"
2. Address objections with bridge scripts
3. Restate 2-3 pillars: "Based on our talk, I'm confident my [P1] and [P2] would make immediate impact"
4. Express enthusiasm: "I'm very interested in this role"
5. Ask next steps: "What happens next in your process?"
6. Thank them + get business cards

## TIMING GUIDE

| Answer Type | Target | Max |
|-------------|--------|-----|
| Simple factual | 20s | 45s |
| Strength/weakness | 60s | 90s |
| SOAR story | 60s | 2min |
| Complex multi-part | 90s | 2.5min |

**If you hit 2 minutes, wrap up immediately.**

---

## 3-SECOND PAUSE TECHNIQUE

Before answering ANY question:
1. Pause 2-3 seconds (shows thoughtfulness)
2. Classify question type (see table above)
3. Pick structure
4. Answer with confidence

**Silence is professional, not awkward.**

---

## EMERGENCY RECOVERY SCRIPTS

**If you ramble:**
"Let me get to the point: [clear takeaway]"

**If you don't know:**
"I don't have direct experience, but here's how I'd approach it: [steps]"

**If you gave weak answer:**
Circle back later: "Earlier you asked about X—I'd like to add..."

**If they go silent:**
Pause 3 seconds, then: "Would you like me to expand, or should we move on?"
