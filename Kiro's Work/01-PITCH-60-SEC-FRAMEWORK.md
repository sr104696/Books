# 60-Second Pitch Framework

## Purpose
Control the first 5 minutes = 50% of the hiring decision. Use "Tell me about yourself" to set YOUR agenda, not theirs.

---

## Universal Structure (REV Intro)

**Duration:** 60-90 seconds  
**Format:** Talking points outline (NOT word-for-word script)

### 5-Part Formula

```
1. HOOK + SUMMARY (10 sec)
   "For the past [X years] I've been [role/industry], specializing in [top REV point]."

2. REV POINTS (30 sec)
   List 3-5 selling points with smooth transitions:
   - "What sets me apart is [REV #1: specific accomplishment/skill]"
   - "Additionally, [REV #2: unique qualification]"
   - "Most recently, [REV #3: impressive result]"

3. PROOF (15 sec)
   One 15-second SOAR story:
   "For example, at [Company], I [Action that produced Result]."

4. HUMANITY (10 sec)
   "What drives me is [motivation/philosophy/relevant interest]."

5. HANDOFF (5 sec)
   "How does that align with what you're looking for?"
```

## Fill-in-the-Blank Templates

### Template 1: Standard Qualified Candidate

```
"For the past [X] years I've been [your role] at [company/industry], 
focusing on [main responsibility that matches job].

What sets me apart is [Quantified Achievement #1]. 
I've also [Unique Skill/Experience #2 that solves their pain point].
Most recently, I [Recent Win #3 with numbers].

For example, when [brief situation], I [action] which resulted in [metric].

I'm motivated by [what excites you about this type of work] and this role's 
focus on [specific JD requirement] is exactly where I want to grow.

Does that match what you're looking for in this position?"
```

### Template 2: Career Pivot / Underqualified

```
"I've spent [X] years in [previous industry], but what you'll find is my 
experience in [transferable domain] directly translates to [target role].

Specifically, I bring [Transferable Skill #1 with proof], 
[Relevant Adjacent Experience #2], and [Unique Perspective #3] 
that most candidates in this field don't have.

For instance, at [Company], I [solved similar problem in different context] 
which improved [metric]. That's the exact thinking I'd apply to [their challenge].

I'm making this move because [authentic reason that shows long-term commitment],
and I've prepared by [courses/projects/certifications].

What aspects of my background would be most valuable to your team?"
```

### Template 3: Recent Grad / Limited Experience

```
"I recently graduated from [School] with [degree] in [field], where I 
specialized in [relevant concentration].

What distinguishes my candidacy is [Impressive Project/Internship Result #1],
my [Rare Skill/Certification #2 for someone at your level], and 
[Leadership/Initiative Example #3].

During my internship at [Company], I [specific contribution with outcome],
which gave me hands-on experience with [job requirement].

I'm drawn to this role because [connection between academic/project work and job],
and I'm ready to [what you'll bring Day 1].

Based on what you're looking for, where would you want me to focus first?"
```

### Template 4: Gap in Resume

```
"I spent [X] years as [last role] at [company], where I [major accomplishment].

After that, I [brief honest explanation: took time for family/health/education].
During that period, I [stayed current by: courses/consulting/volunteer work] 
and actually developed [new skill gained during gap].

Now I'm back and bringing [REV #1], [REV #2], and [REV #3], 
plus the fresh perspective from [gap activity].

For example, my work on [recent project/freelance] shows I'm [current/sharp/up-to-date].

I'm targeting this role specifically because [shows research and commitment].

What questions do you have about my background?"
```

## Critical Rules

### DO:
- ✅ Keep to 60-90 seconds (practice with timer)
- ✅ Use bullet-point outline, not memorized script
- ✅ Lead with strongest REV point
- ✅ Include ONE mini-story with results
- ✅ End with question (hands control back)
- ✅ Match language to job description
- ✅ Show energy and confidence

### DON'T:
- ❌ Exceed 90 seconds (rambling = deal breaker)
- ❌ Recite chronological resume
- ❌ Start with "I was born in..."
- ❌ List weaknesses or negatives
- ❌ Sound robotic (outline allows flexibility)
- ❌ End without handing conversation back
- ❌ Mention salary, benefits, or "what about me" topics

---

## Preparation Checklist

**Step 1:** Identify 3-5 REV Points
- Relevant to job description
- Exceptional compared to other candidates  
- Verifiable with examples/metrics

**Step 2:** Create Bullet Outline
- Use abbreviations and symbols
- Easy to scan mentally
- Leaves room for natural language

**Step 3:** Write One 15-Second Story
- Use SOAR structure
- Include quantified result
- Practice different wording each time

**Step 4:** Practice Until Natural
- Record yourself
- Time it (aim for 70 seconds)
- Practice 10+ times until conversational
- Vary exact wording each time

**Step 5:** Memorize Your Ending Question
- Make it open-ended
- Shows you're evaluating them too
- Creates natural transition to dialogue

## Advanced Variants

### The "Why This Job" Hybrid
When they ask "Why do you want this job?" right after intro:

```
[Your 60-sec pitch] + 

"And specifically what attracts me to THIS role is [Company Value/Mission alignment],
the chance to [specific JD responsibility you're excited about], and working with 
a team that [something you learned in research]."
```

### The "Walk Me Through Your Resume" Version
More chronological, but still strategic:

```
"I started in [industry/role] at [Company], where I [early achievement].

That led me to [Company 2], where I really developed my expertise in [skill]
by [accomplishment with numbers].

Most recently at [Company 3], I [biggest win relevant to target job].

What connects these experiences is [theme that matches job requirement],
and that's why I'm drawn to this role's focus on [JD element].

What part of my background would you like me to expand on?"
```
