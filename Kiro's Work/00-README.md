## Core Philosophy

**Interviews are not interrogations.** They are collaborative business conversations where you control the narrative by:

1. **Leading with your agenda** (REV Points in first 5 minutes)
2. **Answering any question** using universal structures
3. **Pivoting from weak areas** using bridging tactics
4. **Asking strategic questions** that demonstrate interest and control
5. **Closing with commitment** (address objections, ask for job)

Remember: 50% of hiring decisions are made in the first 5 minutes. Chemistry often matters MORE than competence. People hire people they enjoyed talking to.

## Key Numbers to Remember

- **3-5** REV Points (your selling points)
- **20-30** SOAR stories prepared minimum
- **1 minute** maximum for most answers
- **2 minutes** maximum for complex stories
- **15 seconds** for story summaries
- **10-15** questions written down and prioritized
- **50%** of decisions made in first 5 minutes
- **24 hours** to send thank-you email

## Success Formula

```
REV Points + SOAR Stories + Universal Structures + Bridging Tactics + Strategic Questions = Control
```

You're not hoping to get lucky. You're executing a system designed to win even when underqualified.
